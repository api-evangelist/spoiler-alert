May 8, 2018 — Today, Secretary of Agriculture Sonny Perdue invited 2 members of Congress and 11 representatives from industry, nonprofit, and academia to discuss innovative solutions that address the United States’ $218 billion food waste problem. Spoiler Alert CEO Ricky Ashenfelter was among those invited to the U.S. Department of Agriculture’s offices in Washington, D.C.
